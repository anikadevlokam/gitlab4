Standard Technology Stack

• Languages & Frameworks: Python, Flask or Node.js for the backend; React.js or Tailwind CSS for the web dashboard.
• AI & Computer Vision Libraries: OpenCV, TensorFlow, PyTorch, or DeepFace for face detection and embedding extraction.
• Algorithms: Haar Cascades, Viola-Jones, or Local Binary Patterns Histograms (LBPH) for real-time face matching.
• Database: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, or SQLite to store timestamps and attendance sheets.
• Hardware (Optional): ESP32-CAM modules, webcams, or RFID-RC522 fallback sensors.

Key System Modules

1. Student Enrollment & Image Capture: Captures multiple face angles per student to generate unique feature embeddings.
2. Real-Time Recognition & Logging: Scans the classroom stream, detects faces, verifies identities, and stamps attendance into a database automatically.
3. Admin & Lecturer Dashboard: Displays daily attendance summaries, class-wise reports, and student attendance percentages.
4. At-Risk Prediction: Flags students whose attendance drops below a set threshold or calculates potential "bunk" safety margins.
